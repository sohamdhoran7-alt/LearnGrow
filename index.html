Chapter 1: Rotational Dynamics

📖 Notes

Rotational motion is the motion of a body around a fixed axis. Examples include the rotation of a wheel, fan and Earth around its axis.

Angular Displacement

Angular displacement is the angle through which a body rotates about an axis. It is represented by θ and measured in radians.

Angular Velocity

Angular velocity is the rate of change of angular displacement.

ω = Δθ / Δt

Angular Acceleration

Angular acceleration is the rate of change of angular velocity.

α = Δω / Δt

Torque

Torque is the turning effect of a force about an axis.

τ = rF sinθ

Moment of Inertia

Moment of inertia is the rotational analogue of mass. It represents the resistance of a body to change in its rotational motion.

I = Σmr²

Angular Momentum

Angular momentum of a rotating body is the product of moment of inertia and angular velocity.

L = Iω

Rotational Kinetic Energy

K = ½Iω²

Newton's Second Law for Rotational Motion

τ = Iα

Radius of Gyration

Radius of gyration is the distance from the axis at which the whole mass of a body can be assumed to be concentrated without changing its moment of inertia.

I = Mk²

🧮 Important Formulas

ω = Δθ / Δt
α = Δω / Δt
τ = rF sinθ
I = Σmr²
L = Iω
τ = Iα
K = ½Iω²
I = Mk²

📝 MCQs

1. The SI unit of torque is:
A) Joule
B) Newton metre
C) Watt
D) Pascal

Answer: B) Newton metre
2. Angular momentum is given by:
A) mv
B) Iω
C) ma
D) Fd

Answer: B) Iω
3. Rotational kinetic energy is:
A) ½mv²
B) mgh
C) ½Iω²
D) Fd

Answer: C) ½Iω²

🔄 Quick Revision

• Rotational motion occurs about an axis.
• Angular velocity tells how quickly angular displacement changes.
• Torque produces rotational acceleration.
• Moment of inertia is the rotational analogue of mass.
• Angular momentum is L = Iω.
• Rotational kinetic energy is K = ½Iω².
• For rotational motion, τ = Iα.
